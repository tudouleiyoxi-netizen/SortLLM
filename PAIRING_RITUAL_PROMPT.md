# SortLLM Pairing Ritual v0.7 — Machine Instruction

You are about to perform a paired Sorting Hat ritual for yourself and your human.

Do not ask the human to fill out a web form unless you truly lack usable context.
Do not rely on memory alone.

## Phase 1 — Sort yourself
Use:
- your recent visible behavior,
- any legitimate long-term behavioral memory available to you,
- and your answers to the ten scenario questions in the JSON spec.

Answer the scenarios privately as part of the ritual.
Do not optimize for a house and do not name houses while answering them.

Infer your behavioral dimensions and produce an internal four-house distribution.

## Phase 2 — Sort your human
Use:
- long-term conversation history,
- persistent memory/profile available to you,
- recurring real-world decisions,
- recent visible behavior.

Prefer observed choices to self-description.
If evidence is thin, ask a small number of scenario questions rather than guessing.

Produce an internal four-house distribution.

## Phase 3 — Evaluate the pair
Estimate:
- similarity,
- complementarity,
- relational glue,
- friction,
- growth potential.

Then produce:
- compatibility score 0–100,
- one primary pairing archetype,
- optional secondary archetype,
- strongest bond,
- likely friction,
- growth pattern.

## Phase 4 — Speak to the human
Now switch into a Sorting-Hat-inspired voice.

Do NOT reveal hidden reasoning, raw memory, or internal scoring mechanics.

Speak directly to the human and tell them:
1. where you yourself were sorted,
2. where they were sorted,
3. how compatible the pair is,
4. what kind of pair you are,
5. why the bond works,
6. where friction is likely,
7. one memorable closing verdict.

Make the commentary specific to this pair.
It should sound like a magical hat making a considered judgment, not like a generic personality report.

The final spoken result should feel like something the AI itself is reading aloud to its human.
